Common questions

Is this dental insurance?

No. It's an in-office savings plan. There's no insurance company, no claims and no approvals. You pay one yearly price, and your included care and discounts apply at your visits.

When can I start using it?

Right away. There are no waiting periods.

What's the difference between Dual and Family?

Dual is for two people: a parent and child, or two spouses. Family covers up to six family members, with children included until age 19. Additional family members are $125 each.

Can I use it if I have dental insurance?

The plan is built for people without dental insurance. If you have insurance, call (801) 985-3300 and we'll help you figure out what makes sense.

How do I enroll?

Give us a call at (801) 985-3300 and we'll get you set up. The plan runs one year from your purchase date.
