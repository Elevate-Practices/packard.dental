A family of dentists for your family

Packard Dental is run by brothers Dr. Hyrum Packard and Dr. Joseph Packard. We're a family practice in the most literal way, and we treat every patient the way we'd want our own family treated: with patience, respect and zero judgment.

Juggling a different dentist for each person in the house is a hassle. As your family dentist in Roy, we see patients of all ages, so everyone can come to one office and one team that knows you.

What we do for families

  • Cleanings and checkups for kids, teens and adults
  • Kid-friendly visits that build good habits early. Yes, we see kids
  • Tooth-colored fillings when a cavity sneaks in
  • Same-day crowns for the grown-ups
  • Dentures and implants for grandparents
  • Same-day emergency visits for the bike crash or the popcorn kernel

Kids are welcome here

We talk to kids in words they understand, show them what we're doing and go at their pace. A calm, happy visit now makes the dentist feel normal later.

Parents, ask questions too. We'll give you simple tips on brushing, flossing and snacks that actually work in a real house.

Common questions

Do you see children, or just adults?

We see patients of all ages. Kids, parents and grandparents can all come to the same office.

When should my child start coming?

Call us and we'll help you plan your child's first visit. We keep it short and relaxed so it's a good experience from the start.

Can we book the whole family at once?

Call us at (801) 985-3300 and ask. We'll try to line up visits back to back so you make fewer trips.

Do you have a plan for families without insurance?

Yes. Our Family Membership Plan is $799 a year for up to six family members (children until age 19). Each member gets two exams, two cleanings, X-rays, an emergency exam and whitening included, plus 20% off fillings, crowns, root canals and more. It is not insurance.

What are your hours?

We're open Monday through Thursday 8 to 5 and Friday 7 to 2. We're closed on weekends.
