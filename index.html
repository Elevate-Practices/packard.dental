The one-visit crown

Scan, design, mill, place. One appointment.

Most single crowns are designed, milled and placed in our Roy office the same day. No temporary crown and no second trip.

  1. 1

    Scan

    A quick digital scan of your tooth. No goopy impression trays.

  2. 2

    Design

    We design your crown on screen to fit your bite and match your smile.

  3. 3

    Mill

    Our in-office mill carves it from a solid block while you relax.

  4. 4

    Place

    We check the fit and bond it in. Same visit, no temporary.

Questions we hear a lot

Are you taking new patients?

Yes. New patients of all ages are welcome. Here's what your first visit looks like, or book online in about a minute.

Do you really do crowns in one visit?

For most single crowns, yes. We scan, design and mill your crown right here in our Roy office and place it the same day. See how same-day crowns work.

I don't have dental insurance. Can you help?

Yes. Our membership plan includes two exams, two cleanings, X-rays, an emergency exam and whitening for $299 a year, plus 20% off most other care. It's not insurance, so there are no deductibles or waiting periods.

Can I get in the same day for an emergency?

Yes, we offer same-day emergency visits. Call (801) 985-3300 as early as you can and tell us it's urgent. Emergency dental care.

Do you see kids?

Yes. We're a family practice and see patients of every age. Family dentistry in Roy.

What are your hours?

Monday through Thursday, 8 AM to 5 PM, and Friday, 7 AM to 2 PM. We're closed on weekends.
