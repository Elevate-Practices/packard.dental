You won't be judged here

Your first visit, step by step.

Dr. Hyrum and Dr. Joseph Packard built this practice around one idea: dental care should feel supportive, not stressful. We meet you where you are and move at a pace that feels right for you.

  1. 1

    A conversation first

    We ask about your concerns, your goals and anything that has made past visits stressful.

  2. 2

    A gentle, thorough exam

    We check your teeth, gums and overall oral health, and take any X-rays you need.

  3. 3

    Clear, honest answers

    If something needs attention, we explain it in plain language. No confusing terms and no pressure.

  4. 4

    A plan that makes sense

    You'll leave knowing your options and the next step, if there is one at all.

Common questions

Do you take my insurance?

Call (801) 985-3300 with your plan name and we'll check your benefits before your visit. No insurance? See our membership plan.

It's been years since my last visit. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Six months or six years, we're not here to make you feel bad about it. We'll start where you are.

Do you see the whole family?

Yes, from kids getting their first cleaning to grandparents catching up on care. Family dentistry in Roy.

What should I bring?

A photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, and a list of any medications you take.
