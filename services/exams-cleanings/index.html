The single most helpful thing you can do for your smile is also the simplest: show up for your regular exam and cleaning.

Why regular cleanings matter

Even with great brushing and flossing at home, plaque and tartar build up in places a toothbrush can't reach. Left alone, that buildup leads to cavities, gum disease and problems that cost far more time and money to fix later.

A dental cleaning and exam in Roy every six months is how we catch those issues while they're still small. Small problems are cheaper, quicker and a lot more comfortable to fix.

What happens at your visit

  • A thorough cleaning that removes plaque and tartar, including the spots brushing tends to miss
  • A complete exam of your teeth, gums and bite by Dr. Hyrum or Dr. Joseph Packard
  • Digital X-rays when needed, so we can see between teeth and below the gumline
  • Plain-language answers. If we find something, we explain it clearly, with no pressure

It's been a while? That's okay.

Whether it's been six months or six years since your last cleaning, you won't be judged here. No lectures. We meet you where you are and help you move forward at a pace that feels comfortable.

Common questions

How often should I get a dental cleaning?

Most people do well with a cleaning and exam every six months. If you have gum problems or other risk factors, we may suggest coming in more often.

Will my cleaning hurt?

A routine cleaning shouldn't hurt. If your gums are sensitive or it's been a long time since your last visit, let us know and we'll adjust to keep you comfortable.

Are exams and cleanings included in the Membership Plan?

Yes. Two exams, two cleanings and full-mouth X-rays (not including 3D X-rays) are included each year in our in-house Membership Plan. It's not insurance, just a yearly plan with our office.

Do you see kids for cleanings too?

Yes. We see patients of all ages, from a child's first cleaning to routine care for grandparents.

It's been years since my last cleaning. Will I get a lecture?

No. Six months or six years, we're just glad you came in. We focus on where you are now and what's next.
