Partial and full dentures in Roy, Utah

Missing several teeth or a whole arch doesn't mean giving up on a confident smile. Our custom dentures are made to fit comfortably and look natural.

Partial or full?

  • Partial dentures replace one or more missing teeth while your natural teeth stay put. They fill the gaps and help keep other teeth from shifting.
  • Full dentures replace a whole arch of teeth. They help you chew comfortably again and give your face its natural support and shape.

Not sure which you need? That's what the first visit is for. We'll look, listen and explain your options in plain language.

Made to fit you, not a generic set

Older dentures have a reputation for slipping, clicking or looking fake. We take careful measurements, fit each denture to your mouth and shade-match the teeth so it looks like your smile.

Dr. Hyrum or Dr. Joseph Packard will be with you from the first fitting to the last adjustment.

What to expect

  • Impressions and measurements of your gums and bite
  • A try-in visit, so you can check the fit and look before the final denture is made
  • Adjustments after delivery. A little tweaking in the first weeks is normal, and we're here for it
  • Simple guidance on cleaning and storing your denture so it lasts

Want something more secure?

If you're tired of a denture that moves, ask about dental implants. Our doctors place implants themselves, and we'll talk honestly about whether they make sense for you.

If teeth need to come out first, we handle gentle extractions here too. Members of our membership plan save 20% on partial and full dentures.

Common questions

Will my dentures look natural?

That's the goal. Denture teeth are shade-matched and shaped to suit your face, so your smile looks like a healthy, natural smile rather than an obvious denture.

How long does it take to get used to dentures?

Most people need a few weeks to get comfortable speaking and eating. We'll check in during that time and make adjustments as needed.

Can I eat normally with dentures?

Yes, with practice. Start with softer foods cut into small pieces, then work back up to your usual diet as you get used to them.

Do you see older patients?

Yes. We're a family practice and care for patients of every age, including grandparents, from Roy and nearby towns like West Haven, Hooper and Riverdale.
