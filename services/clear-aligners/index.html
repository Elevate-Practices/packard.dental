Clear aligners in Roy, Utah

Straighten your smile without a mouth full of brackets. Clear aligners gently move your teeth with a series of custom, nearly invisible trays.

Book online (801) 985-3300

Why people like clear aligners

  • Discreet. Clear trays are far less noticeable than metal braces
  • Removable. Take them out to eat, brush and floss like normal
  • No food rules. Popcorn and corn on the cob are back on the menu

Clear aligners in Roy are a popular choice for teens and adults who want straighter teeth without anyone at work or school making a big deal of it.

Are aligners right for you?

Clear aligners work well for many common issues, like crowding, gaps and mild to moderate bite problems. Dr. Hyrum Packard and Dr. Joseph Packard will check your bite and talk through your goals to see if aligners are a good fit, or if a different approach makes more sense for you.

What to expect

  • A digital scan of your smile. No goopy impressions
  • A custom series of trays, each one moving your teeth a little closer to the goal
  • Simple check-ins to track progress and pick up your next trays
  • A retainer at the end to help keep your new smile in place

The secret ingredient is wearing them. Most aligners need to be worn most of the day, so they work if you do.

The finishing touch

Once your teeth are where you want them, many people finish with professional teeth whitening. If chips or stains are part of the picture, veneers are another option.

Common questions

How long does clear aligner treatment take?

It depends on where you're starting and your goals. Many cases take several months to about a year. We'll give you a personal estimate at your consultation.

How many hours a day do I need to wear them?

Most clear aligner systems ask for 20 to 22 hours a day. You take them out only to eat, drink anything but water, brush and floss.

Are clear aligners noticeable?

They're far less noticeable than braces. Most people won't notice unless they look closely.

Can teens get clear aligners?

Yes, aligners can work for teens as well as adults. We'll check your teen's bite and talk about whether they're a good fit.
