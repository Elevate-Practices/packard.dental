The one-visit crown

Scan, design, mill, place. One appointment.

Most single crowns are designed, milled and placed in our Roy office the same day. No temporary crown and no second trip.

  1. 1

    Scan

    A quick digital scan of your tooth. No goopy impression trays.

  2. 2

    Design

    We design your crown on screen to fit your bite and match your smile.

  3. 3

    Mill

    Our in-office mill carves it from a solid block while you relax.

  4. 4

    Place

    We check the fit and bond it in. Same visit, no temporary.
