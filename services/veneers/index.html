What veneers can fix

Veneers are one of the most flexible cosmetic treatments we offer. They're custom-made and bonded to the front of your teeth, and they can address several things at once.

  • Chipped or worn edges
  • Small gaps between teeth
  • Uneven or oddly shaped teeth
  • Stains that whitening can't lift

Designed with you

Veneers in Roy start with a conversation. Dr. Hyrum or Dr. Joseph Packard will ask what you like and don't like about your smile, then plan a look that fits your face and personality. Natural, not a row of piano keys.

No pressure and no upselling. If whitening alone would get you where you want to go, we'll tell you.

What to expect

  • A smile consultation to talk through your goals
  • Minimal tooth preparation. A thin layer of enamel is shaped so the veneer sits naturally
  • Custom design, shaped and shaded to the smile you planned together
  • Bonding. Each veneer is bonded securely and built for everyday life

Other ways to improve your smile

Sometimes a simpler step does the trick. Professional teeth whitening is a great first move for discoloration, and clear aligners can fix crowding or spacing without changing your tooth surfaces.

Common questions

How long do veneers last?

With good care, veneers commonly last 10 to 15 years or longer before they may need replacing.

Do veneers take off a lot of my natural tooth?

No. Only a thin layer of enamel is shaped, far less than for a crown, because the veneer covers just the front of the tooth.

Are veneers reversible?

No. Because a small amount of enamel is removed, veneers are not reversible. We'll walk through what that means before you decide.

Will veneers look fake?

They shouldn't. We shape and shade each one to fit your face and your other teeth, and you help plan the look.
