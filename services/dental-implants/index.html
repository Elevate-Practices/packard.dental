Dental implants in Roy, Utah

A dental implant replaces the whole tooth, root and all. It's long-lasting, stable and placed by our own doctors right here in Roy.

Book online (801) 985-3300

Why people choose implants

Unlike a bridge or a removable denture, an implant replaces the entire tooth. A small titanium post goes into the jawbone, where it bonds with the bone over time. Then we top it with a custom crown that matches your other teeth.

The result looks, feels and works like your own tooth, and it doesn't lean on the teeth next to it for support. You can bite into an apple without thinking twice.

Placed by doctors you already know

Dr. Hyrum Packard and Dr. Joseph Packard place dental implants themselves, here in our Roy office. That means you don't get sent across town to someone new for the surgery and then back again for the crown. Same faces, same office, start to finish.

Patients come to us for dental implants from Roy, West Haven, Hooper, Riverdale and Ogden.

The implant process

  • Consultation and planning. We check your jawbone and overall oral health and map out your plan
  • Implant placement. The post is placed with the area numbed first
  • Healing. Over a few months, the implant bonds with the bone to make a sturdy base
  • Your new crown. A custom crown, shade-matched to your smile, completes the tooth

If the tooth is still there and can't be saved, the first step may be a gentle tooth extraction.

Is an implant right for you?

Most healthy adults with enough jawbone are good candidates. If an implant isn't the right fit, a bridge or dentures may be. We'll explain the pros and cons of each in plain language, with no pressure.

Common questions

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most healthy adults with enough jawbone are good candidates. We check your bone and overall oral health at a consultation to see what fits you.

Do you place implants yourselves, or refer out?

Dr. Hyrum Packard and Dr. Joseph Packard place implants themselves, right here in our Roy office.

How long does the whole process take?

From placement to final crown, it commonly takes a few months, because the implant needs time to bond with the jawbone. We'll give you a personal timeline at your consultation.

Does getting an implant hurt?

The area is numbed before placement. Most people say the soreness afterward is similar to having a tooth pulled and is manageable with standard pain relief.
